# Activity: AI Opportunity Mapping

## Purpose

Map specific AI use cases to specific value chain activities and evaluate them on two dimensions: business value potential and implementation readiness.

## Output
AI Opportunity Map v1

## Time
60 minutes

## Process

1. List every value chain activity from your Weekend 1 Competitive Landscape. Add specific AI use cases alongside each activity. Use the capability vocabulary: prediction, generation, synthesis, agent workflow.

2. Score each use case on business value potential (High, Medium, Low) and implementation readiness (High, Medium, Low). For High value scores, write one sentence of evidence. For Low readiness scores, name the specific barrier.

3. Place use cases in the 2x2 matrix. Identify two priority use cases in the Deploy Now quadrant and two in the Invest to Enable quadrant.

4. For the two Deploy Now use cases, draft a two-sentence business case: the mechanism by which value is created and the rough order-of-magnitude financial impact.

DEPLOY NOW Quadrant
1. Automated KYC & Identity Verification (H1-2)
Mechanism: Uses synthesis capabilities to extract and validate data from identity documents (passports, utility bills, business registration) combined with prediction models scoring synthetic fraud risk in real-time. Replaces 24-48 hour manual verification with 90-second automated decisioning, eliminating the 30-40% customer abandonment rate at onboarding currently experienced versus mature market benchmarks.
Financial Impact: With TBC onboarding approximately 150,000 new retail and 15,000 business customers annually, reducing abandonment from 35% to 5% captures an additional 45,000 customers. At GEL 450 average annual revenue per retail customer and GEL 2,800 per business customer, this generates GEL 6M incremental annual revenue with GEL 2.2M one-time implementation cost.
2. Real-time Payment Fraud Interception (H1-3)
Mechanism: Deploys prediction models analyzing transaction patterns, device fingerprints, and behavioral biometrics to intercept fraudulent payments in sub-second timeframes before settlement. Integrates with Space neobank's instant payment rails and traditional banking channels to prevent losses from sophisticated schemes targeting Georgia's $2.2B payments market growing 15% annually.
Financial Impact: Industry fraud rates of 0.08-0.12% on digital payments imply GEL 1.8-2.6M annual exposure for TBC's transaction volume. Reducing successful fraud by 75% while cutting false positives (operational cost) by 25% delivers GEL 2.0M annual value (GEL 1.5M loss prevention + GEL 0.5M operational efficiency) against GEL 2.8M implementation investment with 16-month payback.
INVEST TO ENABLE Quadrant
3. Alternative Credit Scoring for SMEs (H2-1)
Mechanism: Synthesizes telco usage patterns, utility payment history, and POS transaction data from partners (Silknet, Magti, utility providers) with predictive default probability models to serve the 40% of Georgian MSMEs currently excluded from formal credit. Shifts from relationship-based to algorithmic lending decisions, reducing time-to-decision from weeks to hours.
Why Medium Readiness: Requires 12-18 months to negotiate data sharing agreements with telecom and utility providers, establish NBG sandbox approval for alternative data use in credit decisions, and build synthetic training datasets for Georgian market conditions.
4. Trade Finance Document Intelligence (H2-2)
Mechanism: Uses synthesis to extract data from unstructured trade documents (invoices, bills of lading, certificates of origin) and generation to validate consistency across document sets, automating the 3-day manual checking process for TBC Capital's corporate clients. Handles bilingual Georgian/English documents and integrates with SWIFT messaging for end-to-end automation.
Why Medium Readiness: Requires 12-15 months to build training datasets of Georgian trade documents, fine-tune LLMs for local language financial terminology, establish regulatory clarity on AI-processed trade documentation, and pilot with 5 major corporate clients before scaling.

## Scoring definitions

| Dimension | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business value | Greater than 10% impact on a material business metric | 3 to 10% impact | Less than 3% impact |
| Implementation readiness | Deployable within 6 months with current infrastructure | 12 months with modest investment | 24+ months requiring significant foundational work |

## Common error

Scoring everything Medium to avoid committing to a position. Force differentiation.
