# TeamA-DDIT-2026

Team A project for the DDIT course at KIU FIM, Spring 2026.

## Project topic

This repository contains research and strategy work for a consulting-style project on launching a compliant prediction-market platform in the Republic of Georgia.

The working thesis is that a Georgian fintech, potentially backed by a major bank such as TBC, could launch a Polymarket/Kalshi-type platform by combining:

- regulatory-first market design,
- micro-stakes event contracts,
- low-friction mobile onboarding,
- responsible-use controls,
- local event relevance, and
- economically sound liquidity and unit economics.

## Strategic question

How should a Georgian fintech launch a Polymarket/Kalshi-type prediction-market platform to gain regulatory approval, attract early retail users, and reach sustainable scale while managing legal, reputational, and platform-risk constraints?

## Repository guide

- `deliverables/` contains the main course deliverables from the first project phase: project charter, issue tree, disruption diagnostic, industry analysis, and competitive landscape.
- `deli wk2/` contains the second-phase strategy artifacts: strategic problem statement, platform strategy canvas, strategic options, AI opportunity map, three-horizons roadmap, ROI model, and sprint plan.
- `working_file/` contains supporting research files and drafts.

## Current recommendation direction

The project currently leans toward a sandbox-first, bank-backed model:

1. Start with NBG regulatory engagement before public launch.
2. Position event contracts as regulated financial information/event contracts, not gambling.
3. Launch with limited, verifiable Georgian macro and civic markets.
4. Use responsible-use controls, exposure caps, and transparent market-resolution rules.
5. Keep a VASP/crypto-native route as a fallback or narrower strategic option, not the default mass-market route.

## Known open questions

- Final legal classification of event contracts in Georgia.
- Whether NBG would treat the platform as a sandbox-eligible financial innovation, VASP-related service, gambling product, or a new category.
- Required liquidity subsidy and market-maker structure for early launch.
- Sustainable take rate after payment, compliance, market-making, and oracle/resolution costs.
- Whether a major Georgian bank would provide payment rails and reputational backing.
