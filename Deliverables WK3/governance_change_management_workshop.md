# Activity: Governance and Change Management Draft

## Purpose

Produce the first draft inputs for Sections 8 and 9 of the final project report. These sections are required and graded. A governance plan that lists principles without named mechanisms earns no marks on the implementation criterion.

## Duration
15 minutes

## Session
Sunday Block 2, 12:05 to 12:20

## Output
`governance_cm_draft.md` in your team folder

---

## Instructions

### Part 1. Governance plan (8 minutes)

For your recommended AI use case:

Identify which EU AI Act risk tier it falls into.

| Tier | Examples |
|---|---|
| Unacceptable risk | Social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance in public spaces |
| High risk | AI credit decisions, AI hiring, AI in critical infrastructure |
| Limited risk | Chatbots, AI-generated content |
| Minimal risk | AI route optimisation, spam filters, content recommendation |

State one specific obligation that tier creates for your client. Do not write "transparency." Write the specific mechanism: "Adverse action notice for every AI-denied credit application, including the three primary factors used in the decision."

Name the governance mechanism:
- What is the oversight body?
- What is the review cadence?
- Who holds the human override protocol?

### Part 2. Stakeholder resistance (4 minutes)

Name the one role in your client organisation most likely to resist the recommended transformation.

State the specific source of resistance. Do not write "employee resistance." Write the specific threat to their role, status, or income.

Example: "Branch relationship managers whose annual loan portfolio targets are met through personal client relationships. AI credit scoring removes the judgment component from their role and eliminates the basis for performance differentiation."

### Part 3. Incentive change (3 minutes)

For the resistant role you named, state the one metric that must change for their incentives to align with AI adoption.

Write the old metric and the new metric side by side.

## Quality check

Before submitting:
- The governance mechanism is specific enough to assign a budget line and an owner.
- The stakeholder resistance names a role, not a department.
- The metric change names the old metric and the new metric, not a category like "improve accountability."
- Every item connects to a named H1 or H2 initiative on the roadmap.
