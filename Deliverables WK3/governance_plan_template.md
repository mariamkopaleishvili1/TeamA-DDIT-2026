# Governance Plan Template

## Purpose

Document the AI governance plan for your client's recommended transformation. This template produces Section 8 and part of Section 9 of the final project report. A governance plan that lists principles without named mechanisms earns no marks.

## Instructions

Complete all four parts. Every named principle must have a named mechanism. Every governance tier must have a named owner.
