## The seven required elements

Every initiative in the roadmap must have all seven. Missing one element makes the roadmap undefendable in Q&A.

| Element | What it contains | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Initiative name | Specific enough to assign a budget line | Vague names cannot be owned or funded |
| Horizon tag | H1, H2, or H3 | Provides the sequencing rationale |
| Timeline in months | Month range with start and end | Forces realistic sequencing |
| Owner function | Which team or function leads | No owner means no accountability |
| Budget estimate | Order of magnitude ($100K, $500K, $1M, $5M+) | Allows the board to assess portfolio affordability |
| Dependency | Which prior initiative must be complete first | Dependencies are the sequencing logic |
| Success metric | One measurable outcome within the initiative timeline | Without a metric, no one knows when the initiative is done |

## Instructions

### Step 1. List all initiatives (10 minutes)

Pull every initiative from:
- Three Horizons Portfolio v1
- AI Opportunity Map Deploy Now and Invest to Enable items
- Operating Model v1

List without filtering. Target 8 to 15 items.

### Step 2. Sequence and tag (10 minutes)

Tag each initiative H1, H2, or H3.

H1: months 1 to 6. High readiness. Near-term ROI. No new infrastructure required.
H2: months 7 to 18. Foundational investment. Requires H1 prerequisites.
H3: post-18-month options. Activates after H2 conditions are met.

Draw the dependency arrows. Which initiatives must precede others?

### Step 3. Assign owners and budgets (5 minutes)

Assign an owner function to each initiative.
Assign a budget order of magnitude from your ROI model.
Flag any initiative with no corresponding ROI model budget line. These are gaps to resolve in the sprint.

### Step 4. Write success metrics (5 minutes)

Write one measurable success metric per initiative.
It must be measurable within the initiative's timeline.
"Improve efficiency" fails. "Reduce credit decision time from 4 days to 4 hours by month 6, measured by operations log" passes.

---

## Quality check

Before submitting:
- H1 initiatives are deployable within 6 months with current capabilities.
- Every H2 initiative has at least one named H1 prerequisite.
- Change management appears as a named initiative with a budget estimate.
- Data governance appears in months 1 to 6 before any AI deployment initiative.
- Every initiative has a named owner function.
- The roadmap logic can be stated in one sentence: H1 funds H2, H2 enables H3.
