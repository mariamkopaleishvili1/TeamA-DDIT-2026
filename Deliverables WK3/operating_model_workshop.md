# Activity: Target Operating Model v1

## Purpose

Build the operating model section of the final project report. This output becomes Section 5 of the final report. Anchor it on your recommended strategic option from the midterm.

## Duration
25 minutes

## Session
Saturday Block 1, 11:05 to 11:30

## Output
`operating_model_v1.md` in your team folder

---

## Instructions

### Part 1. Process redesign (10 minutes)

Select the one business process most transformed by your recommended strategic option.

Map current state:
- Who makes each decision?
- What information do they use?
- What is the failure mode when the process breaks?

Map target state:
- Which decisions shift to AI?
- Which decisions remain with humans?
- What is the specific escalation threshold where AI hands off to a human?

Do not write "complex cases" as a threshold. Write the specific condition. Example: loan applications above GEL 50,000 or with missing income documentation.

### Part 2. Team structure (8 minutes)

Design the team structure required to execute your recommended option.

For each team:
- Name
- Mandate (one sentence)
- Dataset owned
- Reporting line

Identify the one structural change the client must make before AI deployment can scale beyond a pilot.

### Part 3. Roadmap connection (7 minutes)

Connect your operating model design to your roadmap draft.

For each operating model change:
- Tag it H1 (months 1 to 6) or H2 (months 7 to 18).
- Name the AI initiative it enables.

H1 changes must be achievable with current capabilities. H2 changes require investment before they are possible.

---

## Quality check

Before submitting, confirm:
- The process redesign shows both current state and target state, not only the target.
- The target state names a specific AI handoff threshold, not a category.
- Each team has a named mandate and a named dataset.
- Every operating model item is tagged H1 or H2 in the roadmap.

## Deliverable
Save as: `operating_model_v1.md` in your team folder.
Submit to GitHub repository by Saturday 17:00.
