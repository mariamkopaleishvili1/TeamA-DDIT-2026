# Competitive Landscape Template


Georgia's fintech sector is concentrated in payments and digital banking, with approximately 50 active fintech firms as of 2022 [BTU](https://btu.edu.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fintech-market-in-Georgia.pdf), a $1.18 billion payments market in 2023 projected to reach $2.2 billion by 2027 , and two banks (TBC Bank and Bank of Georgia) controlling approximately 70-74% of banking assets through their combined market share in loans and deposits . No prediction market platform exists in Georgia (the country) as of 2025 — the white space remains total.



## Purpose

This template combines three methods:
- Porter's Five Forces,
- value chain analysis,
- jobs-to-be-done competition.

Use all three. They answer different questions.
